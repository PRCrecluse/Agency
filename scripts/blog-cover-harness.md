# Meridian blog cover harness

Reference asset: `public/images/blog/covers/chatgpt-serp-analysis-workflow.png`

## Locked visual system

- Canvas: 1672 × 941 pixels (16:9).
- Background: very pale ice blue/off-white with subtle paper-like grain and a soft blue glow behind the diagram.
- Brand: preserve the existing Meridian star and wordmark exactly at the top left, including position, scale, color, and spelling.
- Headline: one short uppercase English label on the left, dark navy monospaced/typewriter styling, generous tracking, vertically centered in the lower-middle area.
- Diagram: occupies the center-right 55–60% of the canvas. Use white translucent cards, fine cobalt dotted connectors, bright blue outlines, cyan diamond accents, and restrained coral only when the topic needs contrast or warning.
- Rendering: airy editorial data visualization, mostly flat/orthographic with slight glass depth. No dark mode, photorealism, heavy 3D objects, dramatic shadows, neon sci-fi styling, or visual clutter.
- Safe areas: keep the wordmark and headline clear; do not place key diagram details against the outer 5% edge.

## Per-cover variables

Each cover changes only:

1. The exact uppercase headline.
2. The center-right diagram metaphor.
3. Small semantic accents required by the topic.

Everything else stays locked to the reference.

## Acceptance checks

- Exact 1672 × 941 output.
- Meridian wordmark remains correctly spelled and visually unchanged.
- Headline matches the requested text exactly and contains no other readable copy.
- Overall palette and brightness match the reference cover at a glance.
- Diagram is legible at blog-card size and does not overlap the headline.
- No third-party logos, watermarks, screenshots, or fake product UI.
